# Current paper/dissertation outline

## Working thesis

Behavioral differentiation in an LLM society is a developmental phenomenon but
is not sufficient evidence of functional specialization. Whether private
histories become useful roles may depend on the transfer geometry of the task
ecology.

## Possible structure

1. **From static diversity to `B(t)`** — initially exchangeable agents, routing,
   and information-local feedback.
2. **Clean 2x2 developmental experiment** — private/shared x
   confidence/random, with immutable probes and seed pairing.
3. **Robust behavioral differentiation** — HSE sensitivity and response
   agreement.
4. **The competence gap** — weak Phi/alignment/learning despite behavioral HSE.
5. **Microscopic diagnosis** — exact GF(7) rank, modular aliases, representation,
   explicit-rule execution, and the `x=0` limitation.
6. **Dual mechanism** — competence learning versus contextual behavioral state.
7. **Task ecology as a construct** — causal `L_cd(h)`, comparative advantage,
   retention, and interference.
8. **Research program** — GF(7) as mechanistic/null ecology; generated
   procedural ecology as a preregistered transfer-gated extension.

## Strongest defensible contribution now

A controlled example in which information locality changes robust developmental
behavioral diversity without demonstrated useful specialization, plus a
diagnostic framework separating identifiability, induction, execution,
confidence, allocation, and task-transfer structure.

## What would make the stronger paper

Pre-register an ecology whose measured transfer geometry predicts which roles
form, whether routing aligns with comparative advantage, and whether collective
held-out competence improves. Replicate across seeds and retain null ecologies.

## Claims to avoid

- spontaneous functional specialists already emerged;
- HSE is itself division of labor;
- private feedback is generally better;
- anchoring is the proven cause;
- semantic tasks automatically solve construct validity;
- novelty relative to spontaneous-role work is established.
