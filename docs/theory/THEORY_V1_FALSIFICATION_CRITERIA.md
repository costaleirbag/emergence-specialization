# Theory V1 falsification criteria

The theory is **STRONGLY SUPPORTED** only if T1, T5, and T8 pass plus at least
three of T2/T3/T4/T6/T7, without a catastrophic contradiction. It is
**PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** when predictive ordering is clearly positive but a major
mechanistic law or cross-ecology generalization fails. It is **NOT SUPPORTED IN
CURRENT FORM** when T1 fails materially or predictions repeatedly disagree with
social dynamics. **FALSIFIED ON KEY MECHANISM** is reserved for strong negative
prediction/observation relationships, systematic sharing reversal, a sharp
matched-gain failure with broader routing-gain failure, or repeated reversal of
the predicted stability regime.

`NON_IDENTIFIABLE` is used when the frozen sample contains too few eligible
comparisons; it is not converted into a pass. No criterion may be loosened after
the data are seen.
