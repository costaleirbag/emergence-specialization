# Theory V1 prediction registry

The registry is frozen before social observations. Predictions are generated
mechanically from independent `K^(k)` estimates; no macro result is an input.

Primary tests:

- **T1:** predicted excess early growth orders observed seed-mean growth;
- **T2:** pairwise direction concordance for predicted differences ≥ 0.002;
- **T3:** beta rank response in six ecology×k panels;
- **T4:** equality of `(1-epsilon)beta` matched-gain cells;
- **T5:** private > partial-share > shared ordering;
- **T6:** sealed capacity-law ordering across k;
- **T7:** subcritical/transitional/supercritical classification;
- **T8:** replication across both ecologies;
- **T9:** conditional dominant-mode alignment.

Thresholds are fixed: T1 requires pooled Spearman ≥ .70 and each ecology ≥ .50;
T2 requires ≥75% among at least ten eligible pairs; T3 requires 5/6 panels at
Spearman ≥ .70; T4 requires absolute mean matched difference ≤ .002 per ecology;
T5 requires predicted ordering in both ecologies and private-positive seeds in
at least 6/8; T7 requires ≥75% non-transitional classification accuracy.

The machine-readable prediction manifest is written only after MICRO completes
and before MACRO starts.
