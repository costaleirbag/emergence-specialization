# Theory V1 closure

## Original question

Could a measured local memory-transfer operator predict the formation and
stability of differentiated competence in an initially exchangeable society?

## What was prospective

Theory V1 equations, ecologies, seeds, task streams, checkpoints, thresholds,
and T1–T9 criteria were frozen before the confirmatory MACRO run. MICRO was the
parameterization phase; MACRO was confirmatory.

## What was repaired

After MACRO completion, deterministic audit found a capacity-to-K assignment
bug, full-space rather than niche-centered spectral scoring, incomplete
scorecard implementation, and 31 omitted auxiliary checkpoint rows. The raw
logs were not changed. The repaired analysis uses exact raw completions and
preserves all old sealed artifacts as historical provenance.

## Final status

The repaired scorecard is **NOT SUPPORTED IN CURRENT FORM** under the frozen
global criteria. T2 passes, but T1, T3–T8 fail or are non-identifiable as
specified; T9 is non-identifiable because no repaired cell meets its eligibility
condition. The local K estimators agree, but double-swap superposition is not a
good approximation and the frozen social reduction does not predict the
observed dynamics reliably enough.

## What this does not establish

It does not prove that no nonlinear, finite-beta, router-belief, or alternative
social model could explain the data. Those are questions for a separately
authorized future theory. They are not implemented here, and the prospective
Theory V1 data cannot serve as independent confirmation of that future theory.

Theory V1 is **CLOSED**. Its MICRO and MACRO data are **CONSUMED / DEVELOPMENT
FOR FUTURE THEORY**. Theory V2 is not defined.
