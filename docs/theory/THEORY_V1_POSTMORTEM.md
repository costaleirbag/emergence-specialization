# Theory V1 postmortem

This is a closure note, not a Theory V2 specification.

Observed failure modes: weak prediction/observation ordering, poor local
double-swap superposition, failure of the matched effective gain and sharing
laws, and no eligible dominant-mode test after centered spectral repair.

Questions left for a future principal-researcher decision include whether the
local competence response is nonlinear, whether finite-beta router dynamics
matter, and whether the FIFO retention reduction is too strong. No candidate
equation, new metric, new experiment, or new threshold is defined here.
