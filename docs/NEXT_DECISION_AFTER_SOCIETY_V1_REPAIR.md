# Next decision after Minimal Developmental Society V1 repair

Status: **principal-researcher review required**. This document is design-only;
it authorizes no new inference.

The repaired pilot supports private-state-dependent competence interaction,
competence-aligned routing, and matching complementarity under the frozen
engineering criteria. It does not establish robust realized team-utility gain
(H6), permanent roles, or general useful specialization.

The next decision is therefore between:

1. treating the eight-seed result as a corrected pilot and preregistering a

2. pausing the society track and diagnosing why complementarity did not become
   realized team improvement.

No choice should be made from a single favorable metric. The corrected raw data,
per-seed matrices, routing alignment, matching gain, role persistence, and team
utility must be reviewed together. Do not rerun seeds or tune beta as part of this
analysis repair.
