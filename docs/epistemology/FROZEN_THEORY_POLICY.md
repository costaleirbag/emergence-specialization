# Frozen Theory V1 policy

After the Theory V1 seal, equations, primary metrics, conditions, seeds,
thresholds, ecology definitions, and scoring rules are immutable for its
prospective test. A surprising result is reported as disagreement, failure, or
non-identifiability. It is never repaired by adding a beta, dropping a seed,
changing a threshold, or fitting a new term.

The only permitted flow is:

```text
legacy development data
  -> frozen equations
  -> independent microscopic parameterization
  -> sealed numerical predictions
  -> new social observations
  -> blind scorecard
```

Theory V2 is outside this task. Any revision after prospective data must be a
new document and an independently tested theory.
